# from host vm get access like  as localhost:5432
kubectl port-forward svc/postgres 5432:5432 -n db &
kubectl port-forward svc/redis 6379:6379 -n db &

# from another service inside cluster
From now on, any app you deploy in another namespace can use 
postgres.db.svc.cluster.local:5432 
redis.db.svc.cluster.local:6379 
to connect — this is production-like internal DNS resolution in Kubernetes.
